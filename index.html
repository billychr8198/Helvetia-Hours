    Welcome, climber

    Helvetia Hours turns your study or work time into a trip through Switzerland. You focus, you rest, and every full hour you climb opens a new piece of a Swiss film. Here is everything you need, in plain words.

    The rhythm: 60, 10, 40

    60 minFocus. One climb up the mountain.
    10 minGipfeli break. Stand up, drink water, look far away.
    40 minFondue break. A long rest after every 4 focus sessions.

    You can change all of these numbers in . The 60/10/40 rhythm is the default because it gives you long, deep work and enough rest to come back fresh.

    Watch the valley behind the timer: it is bright morning while you focus, golden sunset during a Gipfeli break, and a starry night with glowing chalet windows during a Fondue break. Your village grows too: every hour you complete builds a new chalet, and fireworks go up over the church.

    Press the mountain button at the top (or E) to explore the valley in 3D. Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom, and tap a cow or the red train.

    Getting started

    1. Add a task. Press Add task and write what you want to do, like “Read chapter 3”. Choose how many focus sessions you think it needs.
    2. Press START. The big button starts the clock. Press it again to pause.
    3. Focus until the music plays. When time is up, the Edelweiss piano tune plays and the next session is ready.
    4. Rest, then climb again. Take your break, then start the next focus session. Each completed hour opens a new wonder.

    Tip: Click the big numbers on the timer to type a new time quickly.

    Unlocking Swiss wonders

    Your focus time adds up. When your total reaches 1 hour, the first chapter opens. At 2 hours, the second chapter opens, and so on, up to 48 hours across two films.

    • Only your chapter plays. When you open a chapter, the film starts at that chapter and stops at its end. You cannot drag, skip or jump to other parts.
    • Locked chapters stay locked until you have focused enough hours. The little lock shows how much time is left.
    • Only focus time counts. Breaks do not count. If you stop a focus session early, the minutes you really focused still count.
    • The introduction is free. Watch it any time to get excited for the trip.
    • Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).

    Tasks

    Click a task to make it your current task. The focus time you finish is saved to that task, so your report can show where your hours went. Tick the circle when a task is done. The small number on the right shows done / planned focus sessions.

    At the bottom, Finish at tells you roughly what time you will be done if you do all planned sessions with breaks.

    The canton map

    Switzerland has 26 cantons, a bit like states or provinces. Open the Cantons tab and tap any canton to read its story, see its flag and look at photos. Tap a photo to open it big. You can zoom in and out with the buttons, your mouse wheel, or two fingers on a phone.

    Some cantons also show film chapters filmed there. When you have unlocked them, you can watch them right from the map.

    Your report

    The Report tab shows your focus hours for today, this week and in total, your day streak, and which tasks took your time.

    Everything is saved only in this browser on this device. Nobody else can see it. To keep it safe or move it to another computer, use Download backup in the Report tab, then Load backup on the other device.

    Can I minimize the tab?

    Yes. The timer does not count down step by step. It remembers the exact moment your session will end and checks the real clock. So even if you minimize the window, switch tabs, or your computer is slow, the time stays correct. A small background helper keeps checking, so the music can still play on time.

    For an extra reminder, turn on Desktop alerts in Settings. You will get a small pop-up message when a session ends.

    If the page closes while a focus session is running, your time is saved. When you come back, finished time is counted.

    Questions

    The music did not play. What can I do?

    Browsers only allow sound after you click something on the page. Press START once and sound will work. Check the volume in Settings, and press Test sound to hear it.

    The video says it cannot play.

    YouTube videos need the site to be opened from the internet (like GitHub Pages) or a local server. They do not play when you open the file directly by double-clicking it.

    The 3D valley feels slow on my computer.

    Open Settings and set Valley quality to Light or Off. The timer is always accurate, whatever you choose.

    Can I change 60/10/40?

    Yes. Open Settings, type new minutes, and press Save. If a session is already running, the new time starts with the next session.

    How do I start over?

    Open Settings and use Reset all progress at the bottom. This deletes your hours, tasks and unlocked chapters on this device.
